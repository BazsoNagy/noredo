Privacy Policy for Noredo - Data Collection: We do not collect, store, or share any personal data, notes, or reminders created within the application. - Cloud Storage & Syncing: If you enable iCloud sync, your notes and data are transmitted directly to your personal Apple iCloud account. We have zero access to this data. - Notifications: We utilize standard Apple Push Notification services. Device tokens are used purely to deliver alerts and are not saved on external servers. - Purchases: All app purchases are securely processed entirely through the Apple App Store. We do not collect financial or payment details.
